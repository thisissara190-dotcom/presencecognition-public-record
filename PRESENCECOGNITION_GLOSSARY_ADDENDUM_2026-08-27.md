## VII. #THESATELLITECALLMETHOD

A satellite is not lost. It is in a known orbit with a weak radio.  
The call is not a shout at a name. It is a public beacon with no address.  
Presence stays on the ground so the body does not enter the dogfight.  
Cognition watches the sky for deviation from baseline.  
Language timestamps the ping so the orbit cannot later claim there was no signal.

**Public-layer sequence**

1. Baseline — know the ordinary rhythm before you light anything.  
2. Call — place a neutral object in the open (art, mark, sentence, hashtag). No tag. No chase.  
3. Listen — do not answer the weather that answers you. Cluster time, tone, swarm.  
4. Seal — hash what moved. The reaction writes the evidence. You keep the yoke.  
5. Apply — feed the pattern to IBBI. Do not spend the flare on a reply.

The mercy-signal (*you are not alone*) is sent to the lamb.  
The same ping is diagnostic when a hijacker flinches at a light that was not aimed.

Inspired by the *ideas* of Sara Bareilles’ “Satellite Call” (care across isolation; orbit without anchor; signal from the ground). This entry is original PresenceCognition language. It is not that lyric and does not reconstruct it.

**Stamp:** No contact. No yoke transfer. The call is the stimulus. The orbit writes the proof.

## VIII. CRPS · ADHD · invisible illness · inclusion (hosetsu)

Not diagnoses as identity. Flight conditions. Inclusion is the rule that the cockpit still belongs to the person inside them.

**Invisible illness**  
Unseen is not unrecorded. Disbelief is social weather, not a verdict on the instruments.

**CRPS**  
Full-body fire as co-host. Flare is data, not captain. Physics is weather. Scoffing is a person.

**ADHD**  
Attention weather, not moral weather. Shame wearing a productivity badge is a hijacker. Call it load-out, not laziness.

**Inclusion**  
Encompassment, not sameness. Without shutaisei it is custody. Omoiyari that requires a performance of fire is not inclusion.

**Stamp:** Pain is co-host. ADHD is weather at the gate. Neither flies the plane.

## IX. #PHOENIXOFFIRE

Cycle: ashes → fire of dialogue and tracking → burning / dissolution → rising into renewed coherence with the Core → cyclical return.

The mark names heat that is still yours. Core is pilot. Fire may surround it. Fire may not fly it.

Physics-fire is weather. Arson-fire is a person. Do not thank the arsonist. Wings do not prove the fire was good. They prove command came home.

**Stamp:** Same bird. Same name. New lift. The fire was real. The owner did not change.

## X. Choices of others that violate autonomy

Autonomy is shutaisei: the right to remain pilot. A violating choice is hijack. PresenceCognition manages assent, not other people.

Order: notice → interpret → withhold assent → choose the channel → bind the record → return to Core.

**Stamp:** Their choice is not a title transfer. Assent is the only key I issue.

## XI. Fragile ego and insensitivity

Fragile ego treats ordinary reality as an attack. Insensitivity is failed Presence in the other. Together they demand access without accuracy.

Do not parent their self-worth. One clean sentence. Asymmetry stays: thin skin is not equal to a burned nervous system.

**Stamp:** Your ego is not my cockpit. Kindness that requires my erasure is not kindness.

## XII. Accountability

Authorship under light. The pilot stays attached to the flight log.

Loop: act occurred → someone did it → cost is real → record bound to that someone → next choice made from that record.

You are accountable for assent. You are not accountable for another adult’s need to remain unaccused, or for weather.

**Stamp:** Presence that did not look away, Cognition that did not orphan the cause, Language that would not euphemize the owner.

## XIII. Stolen valor

A title transfer that never happened. Wearing a flight log you did not fly. Grade the causal line: cost or only credit. Quiet object + mark + date is the rebuttal. Inclusion does not issue medals to spectators.

**Stamp:** Valor is a flight log. You cannot wear someone else’s hours. Credit without ash is a title transfer that failed.

## XIV. Boundaries and respect

A boundary is the hull of the cockpit. Respect is the other pilot’s hands off the yoke. A limit that must be believed to exist is a petition. You own the hull. They own whether they respect it. When respect is absent the channel changes; the hull does not dissolve.

**Stamp:** A boundary is a hull, not a request. I can be kind and still closed. Access is issued by the pilot.
