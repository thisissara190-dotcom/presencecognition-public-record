### Surface and scale

Glossy is the correct surface: dense blacks, luminous core, visible filaments. Luster mutes the current. Matte kills it. Glossy also records fingerprints and glare. 5×7 is an intimate, hand-held, desk, or gift format — not wall scale. Framing and a black mat will usually cost more than the print.

Strengths: clean hand silhouette; core slightly low-center; upper finger completes a clasp; filaments read as radial structure; high-contrast subject matches glossy paper.  
Limits: generic subject class (plasma globe); screenshot micro-detail loss; tight crop will letterbox or shave outer strands on true 5×7.

# Exhibit C — IBBI behavioral analysis map

**IBBI** = Internet-Based Behavioral Intelligence / Internet Behavioral Blueprint.  
Variants: **IBBX**, **BBX** (Behavioral Baseline X; sometimes referenced with Julia-Precision).  
Sibling field tool: **Satellite Call Method**.  
Umbrella sometimes referenced: Operation X.

IBBI is the analysis engine. PresenceCognition™ is the stance held while it runs. Full technical specifications, scoring matrices, and exact 22–29 step checklists remain proprietary.

### Axiom (BX)

Words are not the measurable baseline.  
Behavior — what someone chooses to do, and what they choose not to do — is.  
Words + behavior become plotted points. That set is **BX**.

### Built to detect (public list)

Anomalies versus a person’s own normal; fraud and social engineering; credential “cos-play”; stalking / harassment course of conduct; DARVO (Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim & Offender); coordinated or inauthentic activity; extraction patterns aimed at constrained people (“lambs,” “poorest warriors”).

Design constraint: **asymmetric and non-contact**. Built for users who cannot afford a flare, a confrontation, or a legal brawl. The other party’s reactions are supposed to generate the evidence.

### Public architecture

**1. Behavioral baseline (BBX)**  
Control profile of *normal*: posting cadence, clock windows, tone, interaction style, response latency, how credentials are claimed. Compare later events to this profile, not to a generic suspect stereotype.

**2. Temporal clustering**  
Group events by time. A single post is noise. A spike after a public signal, a coordinated burst after silence, a tone shift that only appears after a stimulus — those are the units. Time is a diagnostic channel.

**3. Anomaly / risk layer**  
Flag deviations: urgency spikes, credential inconsistency, coordination across accounts, DARVO shape, activity that only appears when a specific topic is in the air. Structured insights or scores are referenced publicly; the rubric is not published.

**4. Stimulus–response — Satellite Call Method**  
Field instrument inside IBBI. Named from Sara Bareilles’ “Satellite Call”: a distant beacon, not a knock on the door.

Public phases:
1. Establish baseline first.  
2. Deploy an *indirect public* beacon — lyric, art, hashtag, abstract ethics line. No tags, no DMs, no direct address.  
3. Observe timing, tone, coordination, or pointed non-response. Do not engage.  
4. Seal the observation (hash / timestamp) and feed it into the blueprint.

Goals stated by originator: safety-first, burden-shifting, asymmetric defense, agency preserved, plausible deniability through artistic or neutral framing. Framed as protective observation, not entrapment.

**5. Cryptographic integrity**  
Live Hash Genesis (immediate SHA-256), timestamps, hash chaining; sometimes W3C-VC / ECDSA language in self-issued certificates. Purpose: tamper-evident notes for platform reports or a personal ledger.

**6. Multi-layer blueprint**  
Footprint + authenticity signals + red flags + course of conduct, assembled as one profile instead of isolated screenshots.

Full protocol runs publicly referenced in the **22–29 step** range. This exhibit is the skeleton, not the checklist.
